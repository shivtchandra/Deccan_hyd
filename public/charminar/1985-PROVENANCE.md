# An evening near Charminar, circa 1985

This is an imagined neighbourhood and fictional residents, not a historical map or an archival reconstruction. The era is a creative setting. Exact 1985 street layouts, costume prevalence and shop inventories have not been verified from dated photographs; avoid presenting them as researched facts.

## References consulted
- Hyderabad District, Government of Telangana: https://hyderabad.telangana.gov.in/tourist-place/charminar/ — square plan, four minarets, arches, granite/lime mortar and the later clocks. Reference for the landmark, not evidence for a specific street in 1985.
- Incredible India, Hyderabad: https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/telangana/hyderabad — regional context; no claim that this documents 1985 daily life.
- PixiJS application lifecycle: https://pixijs.com/8.x/guides/components/application — renderer initialization and destruction.

## Artwork and sound
New built-in imagegen assets: `neighbourhood-1985.png` (continuous environment), `residents-1985.png` (transparent 4×2 character atlas). Architecture is a generated artistic interpretation. Characters are separate sprites with foot anchors. Existing earlier assets remain intact. `gestures-1985.png` adds seated cup/sip poses and a grain-scattering pose, generated with the resident atlas as a style reference using built-in imagegen. Its prompt requested a transparent 2×2 sheet: pale sage kurta seated holding/lifting a cup, a mustard-shirt parcel carrier, and a teal-sari woman scattering grain. Only the seated and grain poses are used; the carrier keeps a consistent walking atlas and separate parcel.

The existing opt-in sound synthesizer is reused. It is original procedural sound, not a licensed field recording, authentic speech, or historical audio. No external recording is included or claimed.

## Implementation
One continuous illustration connects the three places. Restricted authored actor routes stay on visible streets. Foreground counter and courtyard wall layers occlude actors. Ambient walkers wait between short routes; the parcel carrier is one persistent actor. View transforms are remembered per place. Action progression is the single clock for poses, effects, captions, sound cues and completion; storage writes follow completion. The old `dhm_charminar_evening_v1` storage is only read, never changed; new outcomes use `dhm_charminar_1985_v1`.

## Verification limits
Desktop and 375px browser checks measure renderer FPS on the available desktop host. These do not establish a 30fps result on a physical midrange phone. That hardware check remains necessary before claiming device performance.
